Explore Like a Local: Eric’s Insider Guide

Five years of hosting, distilled into the spots our family actually loves — with the tips guidebooks leave out.

Day trip · 45 min by tuk-tuk

Kuang Si Falls

Milky-turquoise tiered pools you can swim in, plus a moon-bear rescue centre at the entrance. Go in the morning — hire a private tuk-tuk and stop for buffalo jerky and pineapple at roadside stalls on the way.

Sunrise, not sunset

Phousi Hill

The 360° view over the Mekong–Nam Khan confluence. Enter from the quiet Nam Khan side near the villa, descend straight into the Night Market. Climb at sunrise and you’ll have the misty view almost to yourself.

Scenic walk from the villa

Wat Xieng Thong

The most beautiful temple in Laos, at the very tip of the peninsula. Go around 4 PM when the sun hits the gold stencils on the black lacquer walls. Shoulders and knees covered.

Special evening · book ahead

Manda de Laos

Dinner around UNESCO-listed lotus ponds. Order the Ping Pa grilled fish, arrive 5:30 PM for a “Lao-jito” at sunset, and ask for a table right next to the pond — the best seats in town.

3 min walk · family favorite

Zürich Bread Café

Our family’s daily breakfast ritual whenever we stay at the villa. Flaky croissants, strong coffee, air-con — the perfect kid-friendly break from local spices.

Culture · free shuttle & tours

Ock Pop Tok Crafts Centre

Watch master weavers turn lemongrass and indigo into silk on the Mekong riverbank. Kids can tie-dye their own shirt. Don’t pay for a ride — free red tuk-tuk shuttle from the post office.
